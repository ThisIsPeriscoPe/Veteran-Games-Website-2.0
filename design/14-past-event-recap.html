The Day

What A Day

The 2026 Veteran Games Darwin Heats brought 12 Veteran teams together at the Brigade Parade Ground for a full day of challenge, teamwork and competition.

From opening remarks at 0900, teams worked through a round robin of Veteran Games events across the Parade Ground, broke for lunch on site, then took on the Pool Event before returning for the afternoon rounds.

Every event demanded a combination of physical effort, problem solving, communication and teamwork, with teams only moving as fast as the people beside them.

Across the day we saw the values that underpin the Veteran Games come to life. Purpose. Community. Identity. Excellence. From teammates pulling each other through the round robin to crews coordinating under pressure in the pool, every competitor showed exactly why this event exists.

Darwin Heats Champions

1st Combat
Engineer Regiment

Congratulations to the Sappers, who took first place from 12 teams that competed hard in the Top End.

The day closed with the awards ceremony at 1600, capping off a heat that was contested hard from the first whistle to the last.
