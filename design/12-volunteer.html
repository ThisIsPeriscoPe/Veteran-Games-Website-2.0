Why Volunteer

More Than A Competition

The Veteran Games gives Veterans a mission, a team and a space to reconnect through physical challenge and shared experience.

As a volunteer you help create the environment that makes this possible, from supporting participants and spectators to helping the event team keep the weekend running smoothly.

This is your chance to support Veterans, connect with the community and play a hands on role in delivering a high energy weekend built around teamwork, resilience and purpose.
